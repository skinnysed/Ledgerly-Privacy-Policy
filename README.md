Ledgerly Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 15, 2026

Welcome to Ledgerly ("the App", "we", "our", or "us"). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect
Account Information

When you create an account, we may collect:

Name
Email address
Phone number (if used for authentication)
Profile picture/avatar
Financial & Expense Data

To provide the core functionality of the app, we collect:

Expenses you create
Group and friend information
Payment and settlement records
Notes and descriptions attached to expenses
Device & Usage Information

We may automatically collect limited technical information such as:

Device type
Operating system version
App version
Crash logs and diagnostics
General usage analytics
2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

Provide and maintain the app
Sync and store shared expenses
Improve app performance and stability
Authenticate users securely
Provide customer support
Detect fraud, abuse, or unauthorized activity
3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data.

We may share information only in the following cases:

With service providers required to operate the app (such as cloud hosting, authentication, analytics, or database providers)
When required by law or legal process
To protect the rights, safety, or security of users or the platform
4. Data Storage & Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information using industry-standard security practices. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Some app data may be stored locally on your device to improve app performance and offline functionality.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your region, you may have the right to:

Access your data
Request correction of inaccurate information
Request deletion of your account and associated data
Withdraw consent where applicable

To make a request, contact us using the information below.

6. Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services such as:

Google Play Services
Expo

Analytics or crash reporting tools

These services may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

7. Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
