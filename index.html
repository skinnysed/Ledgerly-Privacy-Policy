Some data may be stored locally on your device. We use reasonable security measures, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us.

Ledgerly is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
